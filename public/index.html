Frog Drop

A multiplayer puzzle game for everyone

📺

TV Screen

Open this on the big screen. Creates a room and shows all players' boards.
📱

Phone Controller

Open this on your phone. Enter the room code to join and play!

How to Play

  1. Open the TV Screen on a large display
  2. A 4-letter room code will appear
  3. Each player opens Phone Controller on their phone
  4. Enter the room code and your name to join
  5. Tap a column number (1-5) to drop your frog
  6. Match same-numbered frogs to merge them!
  7. Higher merges score more points
